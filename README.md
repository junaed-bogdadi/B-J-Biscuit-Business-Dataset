# B&J Biscuit Business Sales Analysis | Excel

An interactive Excel project for exploring biscuit sales, profitability, customers, locations, and changes over time. The workbook contains transaction data, analysis sheets, two dashboards, and a written insights page.

## What the Project Covers

- **Sales performance:** Revenue, profit, cost of goods sold (COGS), quantity purchased, and profit percentage
- **Customer analysis:** Revenue by customer, age group, and gender
- **Product analysis:** Profitable products and revenue by price type
- **Location analysis:** Revenue across cities
- **Payment analysis:** Revenue by payment method
- **Time analysis:** Quarterly, monthly, and weekly views

## Workbook Structure

| Sheet | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `Products` | Product information |
| `Transaction Data` | Sales transaction records |
| `Analysis` | Supporting calculations and summaries |
| `DASHBOARD` | Main sales and customer analysis |
| `DASHBOARD-2` | Change analysis across time periods |
| `Insights` | Written summary of findings |

## Dashboard Highlights

The main dashboard displays KPIs for quantity purchased, COGS, revenue, profit, and profit percentage. It also compares customers, age groups, payment methods, locations, gender, and product price types.

The second dashboard shows quarterly, monthly, and weekly revenue views. The insights sheet brings the main observations together in a report format.

> **Note:** The KPI values shown on the dashboard screenshots differ from the totals written on the Insights sheet. Check the active filters and calculation scope in Excel before comparing or reporting those figures.

## How to Explore the Workbook

1. [Download the Excel workbook](Copy%20of%20B%26J%20Buscuit%20Practice%20Dataset.xlsx).
2. Open it in Microsoft Excel.
3. Review the `Transaction Data` and `Analysis` sheets to understand the source and calculations.
4. Explore `DASHBOARD` and use its available filters.
5. Open `DASHBOARD-2` for the time-based views.
6. Read `Insights` for the written interpretation, checking its figures against the selected dashboard view.

## Selected Observations

The dashboard identifies Shortbread as a profitable product and shows a large revenue contribution from lower-priced products. Its quarterly view shows lower revenue in Q3 and Q4 than in Q1 and Q2. These observations describe patterns in the workbook; identifying their causes would require further analysis.
